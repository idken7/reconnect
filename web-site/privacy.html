1. The short version

  • We collect what is needed to run your account and find friends: your name, email, phone number, cities, and (if you allow it) your address book.
  • We do not store your contacts’ phone numbers or email addresses in readable form. We store a keyed hash of each one, plus the display name from your own address book.
  • We only use location at city level, and we only share it with mutual matches, and only if you have turned sharing on.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. Your data is deleted immediately, and backups are purged within 30 days.

4. How matching works

Matching is the heart of Reconnect, so here is exactly what happens.

  1. You choose to import. Reconnect asks for access to your address book only when you decide to import. If you say no, the app still works, but there will be no matches.
  2. Your device sends your contacts to our server over an encrypted connection. For each contact we receive the name, phone numbers and email addresses.
  3. The server immediately converts each phone number and email address into a keyed hash (an HMAC computed with a secret key that is kept separate from the database). We store that hash and the display name you already have for the person. We do not store the original phone numbers or email addresses of your contacts. Nothing else about your contacts is kept.
  4. We compare hashes. A contact is “on Reconnect” only if their hash equals the hash of a verified phone number or email of another member. Unverified accounts are never matchable.
  5. A match is mutual when each of you has an identifier belonging to the other in your own address books. If only you have them, it is a one-way match.

If another member has imported your phone number or email, we hold a keyed hash of it and the name they gave you, as part of their data. That hash cannot be turned back into your number or address. It is removed when they delete their account or remove the contact.

5. Location

  • We use location at city level only. We do not store GPS coordinates, and we do not track your movements in the background to build a history.
  • You can set your city manually, or allow the app to use your device’s location once to work out your city. The coordinates are used only to choose a city and are not retained.
  • Your current city is shared only with mutual matches, and only if you have switched on location sharing in your profile. Location sharing is off until you turn it on.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by turning location sharing off in the app or by removing the location permission in your device settings. Matching and the rest of the app keep working.

  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off location sharing or revoking the contacts permission. Withdrawing does not affect processing before you withdrew.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and data portability.

To exercise any right, email privacy@reconnect.app from the address registered on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within the time required by law (generally within 30 days). We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

You can also manage things yourself on your device: you can turn off the Contacts or Location permission for Reconnect in your operating system’s settings at any time, and you can log out of one or all devices from the app.

9. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes produced by a slow, purpose-built password-hashing algorithm.
  • Your contacts’ phone numbers and emails are stored only as keyed hashes; the key is kept outside the database.
  • Session tokens are short-lived, refresh tokens are rotated and stored only as hashes, and reuse of an old refresh token ends that session.
  • Phone numbers are verified before they can be matched, which stops people from claiming a number that is not theirs.
  • We rate-limit sign-ins and verification requests, limit who can access production systems, and keep secrets out of our code.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.
