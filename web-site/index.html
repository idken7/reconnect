How it works

  1. 1

    Sign up and verify

    Create an account and confirm your phone number with a one-time code. Only verified numbers and emails can ever be matched, so nobody can claim yours.

  2. 2

    Import your contacts

    If you allow it, Reconnect checks your address book against other verified members. The people you know who are not on Reconnect stay just a name in your own list.

  3. 3

    Match when it is mutual

    A match is mutual when you are each in the other’s address book. Mark who you would love to see, who is neutral, and who you would rather avoid.

  4. 4

    Catch up in person

    If you both choose to share your city and you are in the same one, Reconnect suggests a catch-up. You take it from there, in your own words.

Privacy first, by design

Reconnect only works if people trust it with their address book, so we collect as little as we can.

  • Your contacts are not stored in readable form. We keep a keyed hash of each phone number and email plus the display name you already have for that person. Not the raw numbers or addresses.
  • City level only. We never track your precise location. Location is shared only with mutual matches, and only if you switch sharing on. You can switch it off at any time.
  • You are in control of who you see. “Rather avoid” is private to you and is never shown to the other person.
  • Strangers cannot find you. Nobody can look you up. You only appear to someone who already has your verified phone number or email in their address book.
  • No ads and no selling data. We do not run advertising or third-party tracking, and this website sets no cookies.
  • Delete everything in one tap. Delete your account from the app and your data is removed immediately. See how account deletion works.
