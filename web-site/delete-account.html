Delete your Reconnect account

You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Reconnect and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation, enter your password, and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on all devices.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Use this if you cannot sign in, no longer have the app installed, or have forgotten your password.

  1. Send an email to support@reconnect.app from the email address registered on your account, with the subject “Delete my Reconnect account”.
  2. We will reply to confirm it is you. We may ask you to confirm your registered phone number’s country and last two digits. We will never ask for your password or a verification code.
  3. Once confirmed, we delete your account and email you when it is done. We aim to complete requests within 7 days, and always within 30 days.

If you cannot email from the registered address, write to us from another address and explain; we will do our best to verify ownership another way.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted we permanently remove from our live systems:

  • your profile (name, email, phone number, home city, bio and profile image link);
  • your password hash, and all your sessions and sign-in tokens;
  • your current city and location-sharing setting;
  • everything you imported from your address book: the keyed hashes and the display names;
  • your preferences for each contact (including “rather avoid”);
  • any pending verification codes.

After deletion you disappear from other members’ matches and suggestions.

Timeline and what we may keep

WhatWhen
In-app deletion from live systemsImmediately
Email request: deletion from live systemsNormally within 7 days of confirming your identity; at most 30 days
Encrypted backupsPurged within 30 days of deletion
Error and security logsKept only for a limited period for diagnostics and abuse prevention, and designed to exclude names, phone numbers and emails
Minimal record of the deletion request, and anything the law requires us to keepOnly as long as legally required

Deletion cannot be undone. If you only want to stop sharing your city, turn off location sharing in your profile instead. If you want a copy of your data first, see the Support page or email privacy@reconnect.app before deleting.

Other members may still have your phone number or email in their own address books on their phones. That is their data and not ours, and without an account you cannot be matched.

You may also want to remove the Reconnect app from your device and, if you used a subscription or purchase through the stores in the future, cancel it there. Deleting the app does not delete your account.
