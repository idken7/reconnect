Frequently asked questions

Why does Reconnect want access to my contacts?

Your address book is how Reconnect finds friends: we compare your contacts’ phone numbers and emails with other verified members. This is optional. If you decline, Reconnect still works, but you will not see matches.

We do not keep your contacts’ numbers or email addresses. We store only a keyed hash of each one and the name you saved. Details are in the privacy policy.

To change your choice later, open your device settings: on iPhone, Settings > Reconnect > Contacts; on Android, Settings > Apps > Reconnect > Permissions > Contacts.

Who can see my location?

Only your mutual matches, and only if you have switched on location sharing in your profile. It is off by default. We use your city, never your exact position. Turn sharing off in the app at any time, or remove the Location permission in your device settings. See Location in the privacy policy.

Can people who are not in my contacts find me?

No. There is no search or directory. Someone can only see that you use Reconnect if they already have your verified phone number or email in their own address book.

I am not receiving my verification code
  • Check that your phone number is correct and includes the right country. Numbers outside the US should start with + and the country code.
  • Wait a minute or two, then request a new code. To prevent abuse we limit how many codes you can request per hour, so please do not tap the button repeatedly.
  • Make sure you have mobile signal and that your phone is not blocking unknown senders or filtering messages. Check your spam or “unknown senders” folder.
  • Landline and some VoIP numbers cannot receive SMS. Use a mobile number.
  • For email codes, check your spam folder and that you typed the address correctly.
  • Codes expire after a short time. Always use the most recent one.

Still stuck? Email support@reconnect.app from your account email and tell us the phone number’s country, but not the code.

Why is a friend I know not showing up as a match?

A match needs both people to have a Reconnect account with a verified phone number or email, and for that number or email to be in the other person’s address book. For a mutual match, each of you needs to have the other in your contacts. Check that you imported your contacts after your friend joined, and that the number saved for them is current.

Will the person I mark “rather avoid” know?

No. Your preferences are private. They are never shown to anyone else.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen choose “Forgot password”, enter your email, and follow the one-time code we send you. Resetting your password signs you out everywhere.

How do I delete my account or my data?

In the app: Profile > Delete account. You can also email us. Full steps are on the Delete your account page.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email privacy@reconnect.app from your account email and ask for an export. We will send you a JSON file with your profile, imported contact names and preferences.

Is Reconnect free? Which ages can use it?

Reconnect is for people aged 17 and over. Pricing, if any, will be shown in the app store listing.

Report a problem

To report abuse or a security issue, email support@reconnect.app with the subject “Report” and as much detail as you can (but never your password). We take security reports seriously and will acknowledge them promptly.
